# Validation plan for the U.Psy self-score

Draft of 15 Nov 2026. Complements `submission-pack.md`: the same cohort, the
same consent, one added instrument.

## Why

The free self-score is 12 items written in-house, four pillars of three items
(focus, emotional regulation, recovery and energy, meaning and connection),
scored 0–100. It has never been tested. Today it can only be presented as a
self-check, with no risk or severity wording.

Validated in French and Moroccan Arabic on Moroccan athletes, it becomes
something no competitor can copy quickly: a short, free, culturally adapted
screening measure with published properties, owned by U.Psy and citable in a
thesis. Left as it is, it stays a marketing quiz.

## What "validated" will and will not mean

It will mean: the items measure what they claim, in each language, reliably,
and scores relate to established measures as theory predicts. It will **not**
mean diagnostic: no cut-off for a disorder is sought, and none will be
claimed.

Expected relations for stage 6, stated before the data are seen:

- Recovery and energy: positive with sleep quality; negative with athlete
  burnout (ABQ).
- Emotional regulation: negative with perceived stress (PSS-10); positive
  with well-being (WHO-5).
- Focus: positive with mental toughness (MTI).
- Meaning and connection: positive with well-being (WHO-5); negative with the
  devaluation subscale of the ABQ.
- Total: positive with WHO-5, moderate rather than very high; a correlation
  above about 0.85 would mean the self-score adds nothing to WHO-5.

## Known weaknesses to settle early

1. **Three items per pillar is the minimum** a factor can be estimated with.
   If stage 1 or 3 drops one, that pillar cannot be scored separately. Write
   five or six candidate items per pillar for stage 1 and keep the best three
   or four.
2. **Mixed time frames.** Items ask about "this week" and "today" in the same
   scale. Choose one reference period.
3. **One reverse-scored item** (stress). Single reversed items often form a
   method factor. Either balance them or reword.
4. **A total of four different pillars** is only defensible if a general
   factor is supported at stage 4.
5. **Recruitment through the product** means a self-selected sample. Report
   it as such; add recruitment through clubs to widen it.

## Sample and feasibility

- Target 400 completed baselines, at least 150 in French and 150 in Arabic.
- Present volume is far below this: 5 self-score completions since July. The
  study therefore depends on recruitment through clubs, federations and
  partners, not on site traffic. ⚠️ This is the main risk to the plan.
- No new infrastructure: the study module, consent, pseudonymised export and
  audit log already exist. Work needed: add the self-score as a study
  instrument with item-level storage, and the two-week retest prompt.

## Ethics and data

- Covered by the protocol in `submission-pack.md`, with one amendment adding
  the instrument and the retest. No result is published before the committee's
  opinion.
- Item-level answers are research data: they stay in the study tables,
  pseudonymised, and are never sent to analytics or advertising tools.
- Pre-register the hypotheses above and the analysis plan before data
  collection (for example on the Open Science Framework).

## What the product may say at each point

| Point reached                             | Allowed wording                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Today                                     | "A short self-check." No band may suggest risk or severity.                              |
| After stages 1–3                          | "Developed with a panel of psychologists and tested with athletes in French and Arabic." |
| After stages 4–6, published or preprinted | "Validated in a study of N Moroccan athletes" with the reference.                        |
| Never                                     | Diagnostic or predictive claims; cut-offs for a disorder.                                |

## Sequence

1. Now: item pool and expert panel (stage 1). Needs only the clinical lead's
   network.
2. Then: translation and cognitive interviews (stage 2); ethics amendment in
   parallel.
3. When the doctoral track is confirmed: pilot and main collection, as a
   thesis study with the supervisor as co-investigator.
4. Analysis, preprint, submission.
